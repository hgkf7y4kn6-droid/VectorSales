# VectorSales for commercial cleaning: the offer

**Fixed-price quote-to-cash system, live in two weeks.** Lead follow-up,
proposal and quote generation, and cost comparisons, set up on the client's
own rates and branding.

## Build: $5,000 fixed, two weeks

| Days | Work |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Kickoff: pricing inputs (wages, burden, overhead, target margin, production rates by facility type), auto-send limit, brand, sender. |
| 3–5 | Pricing calibrated against 5–10 of the client's recent real bids until VectorSales lands within their usual range. |
| 6–8 | Lead sources connected: website form embed, Google/Facebook lead forms or CRM webhook to `/api/inbound`. Email from their domain (SMTP). |
| 9–10 | Follow-up copy reviewed in their voice; proposal terms; invoice and payment link (Stripe or QuickBooks). |
| 11–12 | Live pilot on real leads with every quote held for approval. |
| 13–14 | Auto-send switched on under the agreed limit; team training (about 30 minutes); handover. |

## Run: $1,500–2,000 / month

- Hosting, backups, monitoring and updates (one Render service per client)
- Monthly rate tune-up against won and lost quotes
- Monthly report: speed to lead, quotes sent, follow-ups sent, win rate, won MRR
- Copy and sequence changes on request
- $2,000 tier: AI-personalized emails, a second location or brand, and CRM sync

## Who it's for

Commercial cleaning and facility-service companies, roughly 10 to 200
employees, getting inbound requests they can't always answer the same day,
with owners or sales managers who still build quotes in spreadsheets.

## What to measure in the pilot

Speed to first reply, lead-to-quote time, the share of quotes that get all
follow-ups, and win rate against the client's prior 90 days. All of these are
on the dashboard from day one.
