# The 5-minute demo: talk track

Setup: `DEMO_MODE=1 uvicorn server:app`, open the app, press **Reset sample
data**. Everything on screen is sample data and says so. Each step is one
button in the guide at the top; **▶ Autoplay** runs it all in under a minute
if you'd rather talk over it.

| Time | Click | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | (nothing) | "Here's a cleaning company's pipeline. A job that's won, one waiting on a callback, a big one held for approval. This is all sample data. Watch what happens when a new request comes in at 9 pm with nobody in the office." |
| 0:30 | **1 · Send the sample request** | "That's the real website form endpoint. A dental practice manager, 8,400 square feet, five nights a week, paying $3,350 now. There's the card." Point at **Speed to lead**: "First reply in about a second. Most of your competitors answer tomorrow." |
| 1:00 | **2 · Show the emails** | "Two emails already: the instant reply, and the quote right behind it. It was under the auto-send limit, so nobody had to touch it. An $11K-a-month office tower would wait for one click; that's the one in Needs review." |
| 1:45 | **3 · Open the quote** | "Three fixed prices, worked out from labor hours, wage, burden and overhead for a medical space. Good is like-for-like. Better, the recommendation, keeps five visits but makes two of them touch-ups: same result, about $1,065 a month less than she pays now. Best adds floor care and moves restroom paper to a cheaper-per-use format." Point at 🔒: "Your cost and margin. She never sees those." Scroll to the **cost comparison**. |
| 2:45 | **4 · Skip ahead 2 days**, then **3 more days** | "She hasn't answered. Nobody needs a reminder: day 2 goes out by itself. Three days later, the value email leads with *her* savings number. A day 9 'should I close your file?' is queued." |
| 3:30 | **5 · Open the customer's view** | "This is the link she got. She can compare, see her savings, and accept by typing her name." Pick Better, type a name, **Accept**. (Or press **Accept for her**.) |
| 4:15 | **Back to VectorSales**, **6 · Show the invoice** | "The day-9 email cancelled itself. The deal moved to Won. The invoice for the deep clean and first month went out with the welcome email, and she paid from the same page." Point at **Won** and **Collected**. |
| 4:45 | (close) | "Lead in, quote out in seconds, follow-up on autopilot, contract signed, invoice paid. Nobody typed a quote or a follow-up. We set this up on your rates and your branding in two weeks." |

## Questions you'll get

- **"Will it send something wrong?"** Big jobs and anything under your target
  margin wait for approval. You set the auto-send limit. Prices are your own
  labor rates and margin.
- **"Does it sound like a robot?"** The templates are short and plain. With AI
  on, it personalizes the opening from what the prospect wrote; prices and
  links can't change.
- **"What if they call instead?"** Log the reply (one click) and the
  follow-ups stop.
- **"Where do leads come from?"** Your website form, Google/Facebook lead
  forms, or your CRM, through one endpoint.
